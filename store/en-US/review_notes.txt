2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Hallway 24 is an audio horror game made first for blind and visually impaired players, and playable by sighted players too. Most mobile games depend on sight; this one is played entirely by sound and works fully with VoiceOver. The player stays in a hallway for 24 in-game hours, choosing between two doors each time by listening to what is behind them, and survives creatures and sound puzzles by listening carefully. Every screen also has text captions and pictures with VoiceOver descriptions. Audience: blind and low-vision gamers first, and anyone who likes audio and horror games.

3. HOW TO USE IT
- Headphones are recommended (sounds come from the left or right).
- First launch: choose English or Portuguese, then choose a Host (the narrator voice).
- Main menu: "Start the Night" begins a game. In the hallway, tap "Left door" or "Right door" after listening.
- When a creature appears, the whole screen becomes a direct-touch play area: swipe or tap as the Host explains. Every new creature first gets three practice rounds.
- "Practice Room" in the main menu lets you try any creature without losing.
- Every 6 hours a shop opens where tokens buy items (in-game tokens only, never real money).
- Settings: volume sliders, captions, vibration, 3D sound with headphones (with a test button).
- No login, demo account or sample files are needed.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Apple Game Center (achievements, leaderboards, a challenge). Optional; the game works fully without signing in.
- Apple App Intents (Siri and Shortcuts), on the device.
Nothing else: no servers of our own, no analytics, no ads, no payments, no accounts, and no AI services while the app runs. All content is inside the app and works offline.

5. REGIONS
The app works the same in every region. It is available in English and Portuguese (Brazil); the player picks the language and all text and voices follow it.
